## 中文

首个正式版本。给森罗物语：厨房补上摆放、清洗与打水的地方。

### 新增方块

- **柴火灶** —— 三档燃料（木板 / 煤炭 / 烈焰粉），打火石点燃，上方可放本体的炒锅 / 汤锅 / 蒸笼 / 茶壶；雨天无遮挡会自灭
- **水缸** —— 陶土鼓腹缸，四档水位，默认无限水源，缸口空心可以跳进去
- **调味盘** —— 白瓷四格，每格 1024，一格只收一种调料；**打掉只掉一个，内容随物品走**
- **碗架** —— 两层木架，每层 64 个碗或花盆，竖立朝外从左边排开

### 锅铲扩展

- 从调味盘舀调料 → 右键炒锅下锅
- 从搪瓷盆子舀油，交回本体处理
- 从碗架取碗 → **带碗右键做好的锅盛出成品**
- 舀水：水缸、水方块、含水方块、满水炼药锅；再右键水源可倒回
- 带水右键汤锅加水
- **物品栏左上角显示锅铲拿着什么**

### 环境要求

| 项 | 版本 |
| --- | --- |
| Minecraft | 1.21.1 |
| NeoForge | 21.1.256 及以上 |
| **森罗物语：厨房** | **1.6.0 及以上（必需前置）** |

## English

First release. Somewhere to stand the dishes, wash them, and draw water.

### Blocks

- **Firewood Stove** — three fuel grades (planks / coal / blaze powder), lit with flint and steel; takes the base mod's wok, stockpot, steamer or teapot on top. Rain puts it out unless covered
- **Water Vat** — a brown clay belly pot with four water levels, an endless source by default, hollow enough to climb into
- **Seasoning Tray** — a white porcelain dish with four wells, 1024 each, one kind of seasoning per well. **Breaking it drops a single tray carrying its contents**
- **Dish Rack** — two wooden shelves, 64 bowls or flower pots each, standing on edge and filling from the left

### Shovel

- Scoop seasoning from the tray, then right click a wok to add it
- Scoop oil the way the base mod's enamel basin does
- Lift a bowl off the rack, then **right click a finished pot to serve the dish out**
- Draw water from the vat, a water block, a waterlogged block or a full cauldron; right click a source again to tip it back
- Right click a stockpot while carrying water to fill it
- **The shovel's inventory slot shows what it is carrying**
